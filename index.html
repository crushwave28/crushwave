Why it's free

No paywall on love. Ads pay the bills.

Most dating apps lock likes, messages and who-liked-you behind a subscription. Crushwave keeps all of that free and earns from advertising, with firm rules about where ads are allowed to appear.

Where ads can appear

  • One sponsored card after every 15 profiles you view
  • A 5 second ad break after every 10 likes
  • Side and bottom banners, clearly labelled "Advertisement"
  • Likes, matches and messages stay free and unlimited

Where ads never appear

  • Inside a conversation
  • On top of a profile while you are deciding
  • Designed to look like a match or a message
  • Targeted using your private chats

Safety

Dating should not feel like a risk.

Verified profiles, easy blocking and privacy tools are standard on the major apps in India now. Crushwave is built around the same basics.

Verified badge

Members who pass a check get a badge. Unverified profiles are labelled, so you always know which is which.

Block and report anywhere

Use the menu on any card. Blocked people never see you again, and three reports hide a profile until a person reviews it.

Scam and spam filter

Instagram, Snapchat and other social media IDs, phone numbers, links and scam words are blocked in bios and in chat, so everyone stays safe on Crushwave.

Adults only

Everyone confirms they are 18 or older with a date of birth, and the check runs on our servers, not only on this page.

Video chat first

Talk face to face before you meet. A five minute call tells you more than fifty messages.

Your data, your call

Delete your account from My profile and everything tied to it is erased. Chats are visible only to the two people in them.

Crushwave is for adults only (18+). Never send money to someone you have not met. Meet in public places and tell a friend where you are going.
