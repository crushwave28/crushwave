Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Crushwave is a free dating website for adults (18+) in India. We keep this short and plain. The owner of Crushwave ("we") is the person who runs this site. Contact and Grievance Officer: crushwave28@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Account: your email address or mobile number, and the time of each login.
  • Profile: name, date of birth (to check you are 18+), gender, city, bio, interests and the answers you choose to write.
  • Activity: likes, passes, matches, messages you send in chat, reports and blocks.
  • Ads: how many ads were shown (counts only).

We do not collect your password. Login is by a one-time code. We do not collect photos, contacts, location or your device files.

Why we collect it

  • To run the service: login, show profiles, matches and chat.
  • To keep members safe: remove under-18 accounts, fake accounts, abuse, and sharing of social media IDs, phone numbers and links.
  • To count ad views so advertisers can pay for the free service.

Who can see it

  • Other members see your profile (name, age, city, bio, interests) once you create it. Chats are visible only to the two people in the chat.
  • The owner can see members' email addresses or mobile numbers, profile details, login times, and reports. We use this only to run and protect the service. We do not read private chats unless a member reports a conversation.
  • Service providers: your data is stored with Supabase (database and login) and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Email codes are sent through an email provider.
  • Police and courts: if the law requires, or if a court or government agency orders us to, we may share a member's details. We may also share details to stop fraud or to protect someone from harm.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Advertising

Crushwave shows ads from advertisers or ad networks. Ads are never shown inside a conversation. Ad networks may use their own cookies under their own policies.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile any time.
  • Delete your account in one tap from your profile page. Your profile, likes, matches and messages are then removed.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data or ask us to correct it by emailing the contact above.
  • You can withdraw your consent by deleting your account.

Children

Crushwave is only for people aged 18 and over. Sign-ups under 18 are blocked. If you see a minor on the site, please report the profile.

How long we keep data

While your account is active. After you delete your account, we remove your profile and messages. Some records (for example a report about abuse, or records the law asks us to keep, such as for up to 180 days) may be kept for a limited time to protect other members and to meet legal duties.

Security

Data is stored in a protected database with access rules. No system is perfectly safe. If a breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as required by Indian law.

Complaints and Grievance Officer

Email: crushwave28@gmail.com

Write to us about your data or any complaint. We acknowledge within 24 hours and aim to resolve within 15 days. Please also read our Terms of Use.
